what was measured

One card per verdict file. The command is the one that writes that verdict, the numbers are read out of it at generation time, and the note is the paragraph where a builder wrote it up. Nothing on this page is typed by hand: the generator refuses to emit a number it did not read.

retractions and nulls

The verdicts below carry a null result, a refutation or a negative number. They are on the page for the same reason the others are, and they are first here rather than buried: a result that only survives when you hide half of it is not a result.

the soak

One organism has been up continuously since it was started, checkpointing itself as it goes. These are read out of its own state directory, read only. Its control file is never opened by the generator: that file holds a token, and no tool here reads a credential value.
